General Instructions & Scale Reference

The assessment results are conveyed in terms of Mean Percentage Scores (MPS). Descriptive equivalents are assigned automatically as defined below:

96 - 100%Mastered
86 - 95%Closely Approximating Mastery
66 - 85%Moving Towards Mastery
35 - 65%Average Mastery
15 - 34%Low Mastery
5 - 14%Very Low Mastery
0 - 4%Absolute No Mastery
